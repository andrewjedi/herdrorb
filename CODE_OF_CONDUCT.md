# Community conduct

Be considerate, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code without
personal attacks. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or post
sexual content in project spaces.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict
participation to keep project spaces useful and safe. Report abuse using
GitHub's reporting tools; do not repost harmful private material publicly.
This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
